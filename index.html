Your words. Your data.

Reading and dictionary lookup do not require an account. Your last verse is stored in this browser.

Google sign-in requests your basic profile and access to this app’s private Google Drive app-data folder. Only saved entry IDs, review schedules and removal records are stored there. The app cannot read your other Drive files.

A copy of your cards is kept in this browser for recovering unsynced changes. Sign-out hides your cards; “Clear local card data” removes this browser’s cached copies. Access tokens stay in memory and are discarded on sign-out or reload.

Google receives authentication and Drive requests; Al Quran Cloud receives verse lookup requests; Google Fonts receives font requests. There is no analytics or application backend.

To remove cloud data, manage this app under Google Drive settings → Manage apps → Delete hidden app data, then revoke access in your Google account’s connected apps. Clearing local data afterward prevents cached cards from being restored.
